Strategy & communication

Competitions

  1. Cannes Lions Korea & Innocean Worldwide · Busan Region Award

    2014

    Team lead and presenter; an integrated campaign on teen smartphone overuse ("Step out of the 5-inch world").

    Details
    Team
    Three people: planner (me, team lead and main presenter), designer, copywriter.
    Insight
    Smartphone overuse as spatial blindness: people trust the world inside the screen and stop looking outside it.
    Concept
    The "5-inch world", the physical size of a phone and the confinement it creates. The campaign reframed overuse as a loss of spatial and emotional perspective.
    Message
    "Step out of the 5-inch world. Your infinite life awaits outside." (5인치 세상 밖으로, 당신의 무한한 세상은 스마트폰 밖에 있습니다.)
    My part
    Research on teen mobile behavior, the core narrative, the slide story and the final presentation to the jury.
