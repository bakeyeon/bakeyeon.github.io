The proposal that got me hired: Path Unseen

Final project, "3rd Planning" socially-minded ad planning program · client brief: Expedia · Nov 2015 · lead planner

Gap
People want to travel far, but most trips stay short and local.
Research
In-depth interviews with people in their 20s and 30s, Expedia's core audience, on what stops them traveling. I adapted the question flow to each answer.
Insight
"I just don't have the space in my life right now." I split that space into two constraints: no time, and no mental capacity.
Concept
Route-based recommendations from local guides, so people can see a whole short trip before they go, instead of reading reviews of single places.
Message
"Ever feel like leaving the moment you arrive at work?" (출근하자마자 퇴근하고 싶죠)
Trade-off
Small everyday trips do not sell many flights or hotels. I argued for them as long-term brand value: Expedia as the brand for small adventures too.
Outcome
Not executed. The proposal led to my hire at the agency, and route-based sharing later appeared, independently, in Google Local Guides (2019).
