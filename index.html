About

Kai Park

(Hyeyeon Park)

Computational linguist working at the intersection of empirical linguistics and language model evaluation. I study the cognitive mechanisms behind how humans process and represent language, using corpus-based quantitative analysis and controlled experiments to test how linguistic structure interacts with cognitive constraints.

My current work applies these findings to language model evaluation and socio-affective alignment. I turn pragmatic, emotional and cultural nuance into measurable signals that show where model behavior aligns with human language and where it diverges. I own projects end to end, from experimental design to publication, and sharpen them through supervisors, colloquia and peer review.

Working across Korean, English and German gives me a fast start for pilot studies. The longer aim is scalable alignment methods that hold across linguistic structures, not just within one language.
